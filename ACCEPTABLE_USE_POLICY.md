# Acceptable Use Policy

remoteexec is intended only for good-faith security research and authorized testing.

You may use it only against systems that you own or for which you have explicit, current, written authorization covering the targets, techniques, time window, credentials, payloads, and potential service impact. You are responsible for applicable law, rules of engagement, data handling, safety controls, and notification obligations.

You may not use remoteexec to gain or maintain unauthorized access; deploy malware or destructive payloads; steal credentials or data; evade law enforcement; disrupt services; target third parties outside an agreed scope; or conceal activity from the system owner. Do not use real production credentials in examples, test fixtures, issue reports, or logs.

Stop immediately if scope is uncertain, a safety control fails, cleanup cannot be verified, or execution affects an unintended system. Preserve audit records and follow the engagement's incident and recovery procedure.

Maintainers may decline support for activity that appears unsafe or unauthorized. This policy supplements, and does not replace, the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
